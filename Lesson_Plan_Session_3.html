🗣️ Part 6 — Dialogue Practice: Asking for Directions

Instructions: Practice this dialogue. Click 🔊 to hear each line. Then try speaking it yourself!

Worker A Excuse me, where is the storage room? (Xin lỗi, phòng kho ở đâu?)
Worker B Go straight, then turn left. It's next to the office. (Đi thẳng, rồi rẽ trái. Nó ở cạnh văn phòng.)
Worker A And where can I find the cement mixer? (Và tôi có thể tìm máy trộn bê tông ở đâu?)
Worker B It's in front of the main building, near the scaffolding. (Nó ở phía trước tòa nhà chính, gần giàn giáo.)
Worker A Thank you!
Worker B No problem. Be careful — wear your hard hat! (Không có gì. Cẩn thận — đội mũ bảo hộ nhé!)

🎭 Part 7 — Role-Play: Site Tour

📋 Situation

Student role: You are a site supervisor (giám sát công trường).

Teacher role: A new worker on the first day.

"Show me around the construction site. Tell me where things are."

Teacher Prompts (use these questions):

  • "Where is the storage room?"
  • "How do I get to the parking area?"
  • "Where can I find a hard hat?"
  • "Is the scaffolding near here?"
  • "Where should I put the bricks?"
  • "What is next to the office?"
✅ Sample Answers the Student Should Produce
  • "The storage room is behind the main building. Go straight and turn right."
  • "The parking area is on the left. Go past the office."
  • "The hard hats are in the storage room, next to the safety vests."
  • "The scaffolding is in front of Building A. Go past the parking area."
  • "Put the bricks near the cement mixer. It's between the office and the warehouse."
  • "The entrance is next to the office."
